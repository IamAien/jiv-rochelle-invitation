## Complete final edit prompts

### entrance-portrait

Use case: precise-object-edit. Target: the supplied lakeside gate entrance image. Edit ONLY the plants, olive tree, flowers, vines and vegetation material. Replace ALL vegetation with clearly recognizable FLAT HAND NEEDLEWORK. This is a substantial material change. Each leaf must consist of 6–12 separate short olive cotton stitches in an OPEN fishbone/herringbone pattern, with exposed ivory linen visible between the stitches and a stitched central vein. NO solid almond-shaped padded leaves. Trees must be sewn rows of individual small straight stitches, with visible stitch starts and ends. Shrubs are open scattered seed stitches, tiny cross stitches and French knots with linen backing showing. Flowers are small clustered French knots, detached-chain stitches and loose woven-wheel thread rosettes, with many visible separate twisted fibers; NO continuous sculpted rose petals. Bark is uneven rows of small brown split stitches. All botanical shapes have irregular handworked stitch spacing and matte cotton fibers. The weave must remain visible inside and around the plant shapes, not merely in the sky. Photograph/scan of genuine flat folk embroidery on coarse flax: low relief, natural imperfect needle placement, light textile shadows only. The plants must visibly share the sewn construction of the path and clouds. Do not keep the present smooth stuffed leaves, carved-looking petals or uniform glossy outlines. No clay, no plastic, no 3D botanical render. Keep the EXACT full canvas 9:16 composition and ALL non-plant pixels/layout: iron gate leaves, posts, hinge and latch positions, J & R monogram, cafe furniture, lake, arch and paving. Do not move, rescale, recolor or redraw the gate; the website animation uses its original coordinates. Keep the original beige/olive/dusty pink palette and coarse linen backing. No new words or objects. Sharp weave and thread, no blur.
