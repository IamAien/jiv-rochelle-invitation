## Current artwork

The latest two user references guide this revision: fine cotton chain and loop stitches on ivory linen, faded olive, warm grey, beige, dusty pink and lavender. The first scene shows an olive tree, a bench, lavender baskets and modest grey iron gates between rustic stone piers. The second shows a quiet lakeside terrace, a small table with two chairs, and a rose-covered side arch.

The built-in Imagegen tool created the artwork from the references. Portrait scenes used the two latest attached reference images. Each landscape scene was recomposed from its corresponding portrait PNG. The assets describe an embroidered textile style; they are generated illustrations.

## Gate size and opening

The closed entrance displays the untouched complete illustration. Its small J & R monogram is stitched into the artwork; the only interface cue is Open.

The moving leaves use the gate pixels from that same illustration, replacing the previous separate gate asset. Native source coordinates set the crop, hinge positions and unequal widths at the latch. Both axes scale together with the complete scene. During opening, a shaped mask removes the painted gate and the two original gate crops rotate around their outer edges. An SVG alpha filter removes pale linen between the bars while preserving the original grey thread colors.

Portrait crop: x457, y888, width338, height443, latch x620. Landscape crop: x810, y461, width375, height339, latch x998. The camera approach is anchored to that actual gate position. The gentle opening still lasts 8.8 seconds. Loading, skip, failure fallback, keyboard focus, replay and reduced-motion handling are retained.

Implementation: src/components/GardenEntrance.jsx, GardenEntrance.css and src/gateGeometry.js. Historical gate and ceremony assets remain in the source folder but are no longer imported into production.
