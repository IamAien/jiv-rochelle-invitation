## Final generation prompts

### Landscape invitation villa

Built-in Imagegen edit, with `src/assets/invitation-villa.png` as the reference image:

Adapt this existing portrait wedding invitation artwork into a full-bleed WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9 website hero illustration. Preserve the exact fine heirloom silk-thread embroidery and needlepoint texture, warm ivory flax linen, antique cream pearl-trimmed silk curtains, sage green foliage, dusty rose roses, lavender wisteria, and French villa aesthetic. Recompose, do not stretch the original. The elegantly gathered open silk curtains should frame the FAR LEFT and FAR RIGHT edges with pearl swags across the top 15%. Preserve a large clean EMPTY warm ivory linen space in the central 48% of the canvas from 22% to 57% height, where live wedding names and date will be added by the website. Place the charming symmetrical French villa, garden, and pale aqua fountain in the LOWER 35% of the image. Use delicate cream and dusty pink embroidered roses to frame both bottom corners, with leafy garden extending across the entire bottom edge. Full scene fills the wide canvas, no blank outer gutters. No text, no letters, no monograms, no watermark, no borders, no UI, no phone frame. Keep the illustration's restrained antique palette and tactile raised textile detail. High-quality panoramic embroidered wedding stationery artwork.

### Invitation villa

Use case: illustration-story. Create one original high-end wedding invitation illustration asset, tall portrait 2:3 format. Image only, NOT a phone mockup, NOT a website screenshot. Reference aesthetic: heirloom French embroidered wedding stationery, raised silk-thread embroidery and fine needlepoint on warm ivory flax linen, romantic, softly dimensional and tactile, muted sage, antique cream, dusty rose, pale lavender and subtle antique gold. Composition: ivory silk theater curtains elegantly held open at the far left and far right edges, with a richly draped three-swag ivory valance across the top 16%, tiny pearl bead trims along the swag and inner curtain edge. Preserve a very large EMPTY warm ivory linen area from 22% to 58% height in the central 70% of the image for website text added later. At the bottom 38%, a charming symmetrical French villa with creamy stone walls, dusty rose tiled roof, sage window shutters, lavender climbing wisteria, topiary garden, and a small pale aqua fountain in front. Bottom corners of image framed by embroidered dusty rose and cream roses with sage leaves. No text, no letters, no monograms, no watermarks, no border, no phone. Scene symmetrical with almost orthographic flat fine-art elevation, real textile stitch detail, beautifully restrained antique palette. Fill canvas edge to edge. The empty middle must remain clean and usable.

### Pearl floral garland

Use case: illustration-story. A single original horizontal floral garland asset for a refined heirloom wedding invitation website, isolated on a truly transparent background. Wide landscape composition about 3:1. Fine raised silk-thread embroidery and needlepoint, tactile stitched craft aesthetic, subtle shadows. An elegant gently arching vine of muted sage green leaves with dusty mauve rosebuds, antique cream roses and delicate cascading pale lavender wisteria clusters near the left and right, connected by graceful looping strands of tiny ivory pearls. Restrained, delicate, airy and symmetrical. Pale antique gold vine. ALL flowers and pearls kept within the canvas with clear transparent padding along edges. No text, no monograms, no frames, no containers, no lettering, no watermark. High detail, perfect for a luxury wedding invitation, muted colors and softly dimensional soft studio lighting.

### Closed ivory curtains

Use case: illustration-story. Original seamless-looking closed ivory silk theater curtains, portrait 2:3 composition, front elevation, extremely fine heirloom silk-thread embroidery needlepoint textile style. Two symmetrical full-height curtain panels meet at a vertical center seam, trimmed along that seam with tiny antique ivory pearls. Many deep vertical soft luxurious folds, subtle woven flax texture, warm soft ivory and antique cream, soft dimensional lighting from upper left. ONLY full height vertical pleated curtain fabric, NO draped top swags, NO valance, NO tiebacks, NO floor, NO building, NO objects, NO borders, NO text or watermark. Fill every part of the image edge to edge with the ivory silk curtain. Designed as an animation asset split down the center on a luxurious wedding invitation website. Restrained warm antique cream palette, tactile fine stitched detail, flat orthographic front-facing view.
